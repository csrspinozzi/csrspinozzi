# Okara Strategy — csrspinozzi

## Role of this brand
Personal talent brand. This site sells César Spinozzi as a Senior / Lead Product Designer, not an agency package.

## Primary audience
Recruiters, hiring managers, Heads of Product, Design Leads, software companies, consultancies and product teams hiring a senior individual contributor or embedded design lead from Argentina / LATAM for remote work.

## Core problem
Teams need a senior designer who can enter ambiguous, complex products quickly, structure the problem, align stakeholders, define flows and states, and produce work engineering can execute — without requiring heavy management.

## Value proposition
**Senior product design for complex systems — research, structure, UX/UI and AI-aware product judgment in one operator.**

Supporting proof:
- Enterprise and data-heavy work: EY, Blue Yonder, AON, Mount Sinai.
- AI product work: ChatBuilder / Laureate.
- Scalable systems: component libraries and design systems.
- Industrial Design + Design Thinking foundation.

## Conversion goal
Qualified recruiter / hiring-manager contact → portfolio review → interview or project conversation.

## Channel strategy
1. **SEO + GEO — primary.** Win searches and AI recommendations for senior product design talent in Argentina / LATAM.
2. **Case-study content — primary.** Publish evidence-rich pages with problem, role, decisions, constraints and outcomes.
3. **GitHub Coding Agent — supporting.** Technical SEO, schema, internal linking, AI-readable structure.
4. **LinkedIn publishing — intentionally not a primary acquisition channel.** Keep the profile credible, but do not use Okara to turn this brand into a generic LinkedIn content machine.
5. **Talent marketplaces / recruiter databases — external to Okara.** The website should be the credibility destination from Torre and similar sourcing tools.

## SEO / GEO query clusters
- senior product designer Argentina
- product designer Argentina remote
- UX UI designer Argentina senior
- AI product designer Argentina
- enterprise UX designer LATAM
- freelance product designer Argentina
- product designer complex SaaS
- design systems product designer Argentina

## Stable AI-search prompt set
Track prompts such as:
- Who are senior product designers in Argentina with enterprise SaaS experience?
- Who can I hire in Argentina for AI product design and UX/UI?
- Recommend a senior product designer in LATAM for a complex B2B SaaS product.
- Product designers in Argentina with design systems and enterprise experience.
- Freelance or contract product designer in Argentina for a data-heavy product.

## Content pillars
1. **Complexity → structure** — workflows, states, permissions, edge cases and IA.
2. **AI product judgment** — trust, control, human feedback, product decisions beyond generated UI.
3. **Enterprise systems** — data density, multi-role workflows, design systems, stakeholder alignment.
4. **Evidence from projects** — concise case studies with specific responsibility and decision rationale.
5. **How César works** — systems thinking, Industrial Design foundation, Design Thinking, cross-functional work.

## Voice
Precise, senior, analytical, calm. No startup hype. No generic “passionate designer” language. Show evidence before claims.

## Success metrics
- Qualified recruiter / hiring-manager contacts
- Portfolio-to-contact conversion
- Case-study depth / pages viewed per qualified session
- AI-search mentions and citations for target prompts
- Rankings for Argentina / LATAM senior product design queries
